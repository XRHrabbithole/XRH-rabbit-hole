// About

XRH (The Rabbit Hole) is a community-born meme token on the XRP Ledger. It started as a Telegram group diving into conspiracy theories, upcoming tech, and everything in between. Now it’s a playful on-chain experiment with farming, NFTs, and daily rewards.
